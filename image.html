KK (born Krishnakumar Kunnath; 23 August 1968 – 31 May 2022) was an Indian playback singer. He sang in multiple languages, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, and Gujarati. KK was known for his versatile singing style and has contributed to numerous hit songs in the Indian music industry.

He began his career in the late 1990s and quickly gained popularity with his soulful voice and emotive performances. Some of his most popular songs include "Tadap Tadap," "Yaaron," "Pal," and "Tu Aashiqui Hai." KK's music has left a lasting impact on the Indian music scene, and he is remembered fondly by fans and fellow artists alike.

Tragically, KK passed away on 31 May 2022, leaving behind a legacy of memorable songs and performances. His contributions to the music industry continue to be celebrated, and his voice remains cherished by listeners around the world.
